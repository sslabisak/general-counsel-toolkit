# General Counsel Toolkit

Working templates for running the legal function of a growing company with one lawyer. I built and use these as General Counsel and as outside counsel. They are the scaffolding around the legal work: the documents that make advice fast, consistent, and easy for a founder to act on.

## What is here

| Template | Use it when | What it produces |
|---|---|---|
| [Dispute assessment](templates/01-dispute-assessment.md) | A demand, a claim, or a threat arrives, or the company is deciding whether to bring one | One page: what it is worth, what it will cost, and the odds, with a recommendation |
| [Outside counsel screening](templates/02-outside-counsel-screening.md) | A matter needs counsel in another jurisdiction | Eleven questions, a comparison grid, and an intake brief so the engagement starts on your terms |
| [Phased litigation budget](templates/03-phased-litigation-budget.md) | Before any engagement or filing | A budget by phase with a decision point at the end of each ([CSV](templates/03-phased-litigation-budget.csv)) |
| [IP ownership checklist](templates/04-ip-ownership-checklist.md) | Onboarding as counsel, before a financing, or before a sale | Proof that the company owns what it has built and bought, and a list of gaps to close |
| [Contract intake and playbook](templates/05-contract-intake-playbook.md) | Routine contracts are waiting on a lawyer | An intake form, a triage rule, and a playbook of preferred and fallback positions |
| [Terms rollout checklist](templates/06-terms-rollout-checklist.md) | Customer terms or a privacy policy are changing | Notice, acceptance, and the records that make the change enforceable |
| [Public claims file](templates/07-public-claims-file.md) | Marketing makes claims about users, results, security, or uptime | One line per claim with its support, owner, and review date ([CSV](templates/07-public-claims-file.csv)) |
| [Platform dependency register](templates/08-platform-dependency-register.md) | The product depends on another company's platform, API, or app store | One page per dependency: the rules that apply, the risk, and the plan if access changes |

## How I use them

1. **Decisions belong to the business.** Each template ends in a recommendation and a named decision for the founder or an executive. Legal risk is one input, stated plainly, next to cost and odds.
2. **Write it down once.** A position taken in one negotiation goes into the playbook, so the next contract of that type does not need a lawyer.
3. **Build the record before the dispute.** Acceptance logs, version histories, and claim support are cheap to keep and expensive to reconstruct.
4. **Price before work.** Every dispute gets a phased budget before anyone is engaged.
