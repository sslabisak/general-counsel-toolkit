# Platform Dependency Register

If a product reaches its users through someone else's platform, API, or app store, that company's rules are part of the product's legal foundation. One page per dependency. Review whenever a feature ships or a platform changes its terms.

## Summary

| Platform | What we use it for | How we connect | Governing terms | Risk if access changes | Owner | Last reviewed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | Official API / app store / user-authorized session / other | | High / Medium / Low | | |

## One page per dependency

### 1. Connection

- What the product does with this platform, in one paragraph
- How the connection works technically, in terms a non-engineer can follow
- Whether the method is one the platform offers for this purpose
- What credentials or permissions are involved, and who holds them

### 2. Rules

- The terms that apply to us, with links and the date last read
- The terms that apply to our users, and whether our product could cause a user to breach them
- Required disclosures, consents, or privacy policy language
- Limits on storage, retention, transfer to third parties, AI use, and human access
- Any review, verification, or security assessment the platform requires, its status, and its renewal date

### 3. What we tell users

- Where our terms and privacy policy describe this connection, and whether the description is accurate today
- Any risk to the user's own account, and where we disclose it

- Who watches this platform's policy changes
- Who the platform contact is, if there is one
